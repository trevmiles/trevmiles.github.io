    About

    I lead Data & Analytics at Vodafone Papua New Guinea, overseeing the data warehouse, analytics and reporting capabilities that support business operations and executive decision-making. From network and customer intelligence to commercial performance, my team builds the data solutions that give stakeholders a clearer picture of the business.

    I work across the data lifecycle, from querying and transforming large datasets in Greenplum and Oracle to developing automated Python workflows and maintaining data quality. Beyond the engineering, I focus on the end product: well-structured, visually polished reporting that transforms raw extracts into meaningful business intelligence.

    My career in technology began in 2018 with the founding of TwoFour Technologies, my own web development venture. I went on to spend nearly four years at Kina Bank, progressing from software engineering into data engineering, before joining Vodafone Papua New Guinea in 2023, where I now lead Data & Analytics.

    In September 2026, I began an MSc in Data Science and Analytics at Brunel University of London as a Chevening Scholar. This next chapter builds on my experience in software and data, while deepening my expertise in advanced analytics and emerging technologies. I’m particularly interested in how data, digital infrastructure and technology can drive innovation, strengthen institutions and create opportunities across the Pacific.
